Privacy Policy

Kapıyı Açma

This privacy policy applies to the mobile game Kapıyı Açma, developed and published by MK Android Studio.

Effective date: August 27, 2026

Information We Collect

The game asks users to provide a username. The username is stored on the user's device and may be submitted together with game information such as score, round, level, game mode, and submission date for leaderboard features.

Users should not enter their real name, email address, phone number, or other personal information as their username. Leaderboard usernames and scores may be visible to other players.

Advertising

The game displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob, including rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location, app interactions, advertisement interactions, and diagnostic data.

This information may be used to provide and measure advertisements, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain services, and, where permitted, personalize advertising.

For more information, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Local Storage

The game uses local device storage to remember information such as the username entered by the user and game-related preferences. Removing the application or clearing its application data may remove locally stored information.

How We Use Information

Information processed by the game may be used to:

  • Provide gameplay and leaderboard features
  • Display usernames and scores on the leaderboard
  • Maintain and improve the game
  • Display and measure advertisements
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, and technical problems

Data Sharing

Game information may be processed by service providers used to operate the game and its advertising features. We do not sell users' directly identifying personal information.

Data Retention and Deletion

Leaderboard information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the leaderboard and operate the game. Users may request the deletion of their submitted username and score information by contacting us at the email address below and providing the username used in the game.

Children's Privacy

Users should not submit real names or other personal information through the username field. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has submitted personal information, please contact us so that we can review and, where appropriate, delete the information.

Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information processed by the game. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the game's features, services, or legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
